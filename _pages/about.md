Hi, I'm Jeremy.

Previously, I focused on optimizing model deployment through quantization and
pruning.

Before joining Cruise, I earned a double major in Computer Science and Math at the University of Waterloo, where I focused on optimization.
During my time there, I also interned at Apple, working on their autonomous driving project.
